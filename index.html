I craft learning experiences where knowledge becomes muscle memory

Hi, I’m Shamia, an instructional designer and eLearning developer. I design and build interactive and gamified eLearning for all audiences, depending on their needs.

My approach

Instructional design is how I turn what people need to learn into a clear plan that helps them learn it. I start by finding out who the learners are and what they struggle with. Then I set clear goals, choose activities and tests that build the right skills, and plan the flow of the lessons.

eLearning development is where I turn that plan into a working online course. I use tools like Articulate Storyline 360 to build the interactions, scenarios, simulations and quizzes, then I test the course and package it so it runs on any learning platform (LMS).

Without the design, a course is just content on slides. Without the build, a good plan stays on paper. I do both, so what I plan is what learners actually get.

Customer Training Refresher course

De-escalating an angry customer

The problem: Frontline customer service representatives struggle with handling emotionally charged customer interactions, which results in an increase in escalations to supervisors.

The scenario

The learner plays Alex, a customer service representative at a fictional online retailer called Northfield Retail. The company is generic enough to feel realistic without tying it to a real brand.

The customer: Priyah Shah

  • She ordered a laptop for her son's school exams and paid for expedited shipping.
  • It arrives 4 days late, after the exam period.
  • She has already been transferred once today, so her first-call frustration is compounding.
  • She is not naturally aggressive. She is a normally reasonable person who is at her limit, which makes her more realistic and relatable than an over-the-top "irate customer".

How I solved it: A branching story in Storyline 360 where learners make decisions and get feedback straight away.
