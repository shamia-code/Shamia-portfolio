How I work

A short, consistent process from brief to published course.

01

Analyze & design

Clarify the performance gap, define objectives, and storyboard the learning flow before opening any tool.

02

Build & prototype

Develop in Storyline with a working prototype early, so stakeholders can react to something real, not a script.

03

Test & refine

Run it with real users, fix friction points, then publish and track how it performs after launch.

About

My background spans corporate compliance, software onboarding, and sales enablement, usually working closely with subject matter experts who know the content but not the tools.
